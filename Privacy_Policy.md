# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how [Bot Name] ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and manages information when users interact with its features on Discord.

We aim to minimize the collection of personal information and use collected data only to provide and maintain the Bot's functionality.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the Bot may collect and process:

**Discord Information**
- Discord user IDs associated with submissions or moderation activities.
- Discord server IDs and channel IDs required for server-specific functionality.
- Information explicitly provided through Bot commands and interactions.

**Fanfiction Recommendation Information**
- Submitted fanfiction URLs.
- Story titles, authors, fandoms, and other publicly available metadata.
- User-provided descriptions, tags, ratings, and recommendations.
- Submission timestamps.

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, private messages unrelated to its functionality, or other unnecessary personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Collected information may be used to:

- Process and organize fanfiction recommendations.
- Store recommendations in a spreadsheet or database.
- Display and retrieve previously submitted recommendations.
- Identify duplicate submissions.
- Support moderation and prevent abuse.
- Maintain and improve Bot functionality.

We do not sell personal information or use collected data for targeted advertising.

## 4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

The Bot may use Google Sheets or another database service to store recommendation records and related information.

Information is also processed through Discord's platform as necessary to operate the Bot.

Access to stored data will be limited according to the Bot's configuration and the permissions granted to authorized administrators.

Submitted recommendations may be visible to other Discord users or server members depending on the Bot's functionality and server settings.

## 5. Data Retention

Recommendation records may be retained while they are needed for the Bot's functionality.

Associated Discord identifiers may be retained for moderation, attribution, or duplicate detection where those features are enabled.

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their Discord account, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate operational requirements.

Deletion of personal information does not necessarily require deletion of non-personal recommendation information.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information, including restricting access to Bot credentials and external storage systems.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers necessary to operate the Bot, such as Discord, Google, and hosting providers.

Recommendation data may also be displayed to other users as part of the Bot's intended functionality.

Information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect the security of the Bot and its users.

## 8. Third-Party Links

Recommendations may contain links to external fanfiction websites.

We do not control the privacy practices or content of these websites. Users should review the respective websites' privacy policies before accessing them.
